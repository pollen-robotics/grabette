First steps to get started with your new Grabette

Power on your Grabette

  1. Press once
  2. Press a second time and hold
  3. Release when the blue light appears

Give the device a moment to start up before going to the next step.

In the dashboard

Make sure you're on the same network as Grabette.

  1. Log in to your HuggingFace account : by one-click OAuth or with a token
  2. Take a look at the view of both cameras and the gripper joint data
  3. Make a test recording to check everything is working fine.
  4. Access the Grabette Fleet space.

Finish WiFi setup — the link appears once the Grabette reports its address.
